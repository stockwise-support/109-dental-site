Family dentistry in Queen Alexandra

109 Dental is a family dental clinic in Suite 204 at 7125 109 Street NW in Edmonton's Queen Alexandra neighbourhood, near Strathcona and the University of Alberta. We see children and adults for checkups, fillings, and ongoing care.

Call (780) 435-5300 Book / request an appointment

What family care includes

Exams, cleanings, fillings, and the everyday work that keeps kids and adults comfortable. When someone needs a crown, implant, or Invisalign, we discuss that on a dedicated visit.

We accept new patients and the Canadian Dental Care Plan. Direct billing is available for many insurance plans.

Your first visit

Bring a medication list, insurance or CDCP details, and any recent dental records. Tell us about pain, sensitivity, previous treatment, or anything that makes dental visits difficult. Call ahead if you want to arrange record transfer.

Checkups, cleaning, and fillings

A checkup and a cleaning serve different purposes. An exam helps identify concerns; a hygiene appointment addresses plaque and tartar. Ask what is included in the appointment you book. If a filling or other treatment is recommended, ask about the options and estimate before deciding.

Planning ongoing care

Appointment frequency should fit your needs. Ask your dentist when to return and what to focus on at home. If several family members need visits, call to discuss available times.

Questions

Can the whole family come here?

Yes. We see children, students, and adults. Book each person a time that fits, or ask about same-day family blocks when the schedule allows.

Do you offer regular cleanings?

Yes. Hygiene visits are a core part of family dentistry. Frequency depends on your gums and history.
