Patient guide

Digital scans and planning dental treatment

If a digital scan is part of your proposed treatment, ask what it records and how it will be used.

Know what your appointment includes

When booking an aligner or restorative consultation, ask which records are needed and whether existing records can be transferred. Confirm whether a scan, photographs, or x-rays are included in the estimate.

Questions about iTero

If you were referred for an iTero scan, tell the office the purpose of the referral and confirm current equipment availability before booking. A scan or image should be interpreted as part of a full assessment, not as a treatment promise.

Understand the proposed result

Ask the dentist to explain any visual treatment simulation, its limitations, and which decisions still depend on examining your teeth and bite.

Planning a visit to 109 Dental

Call (780) 435-5300 to ask which appointment to book and whether the care you need is available at the clinic. Bring any referral, recent dental records, medication list, and coverage details. An assessment helps determine the next step, including whether a referral is needed.
