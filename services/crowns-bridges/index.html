Crowns and bridges in Edmonton

A cracked, heavily filled, or missing tooth may need a crown or a bridge. We explain the difference after we see the tooth and your bite.

Call (780) 435-5300 Book / request an appointment

Crowns versus bridges

A crown covers one tooth. A bridge replaces a gap by using neighbouring teeth. Implants are another way to fill a space. We compare those options with cost and upkeep in mind.

Before treatment

Ask how the proposed restoration would be supported, what happens to the neighbouring teeth, how it is cleaned, and what follow-up is needed. Bring insurance details and request an estimate before treatment.

Questions to ask when comparing a bridge with other options

Which problem are you looking to address?

A crown restores an existing tooth; a bridge replaces missing teeth. Each has different preparation, cleaning and cost considerations.

Questions

How long does a crown take?

Often more than one visit. Timing depends on the tooth and the lab work. We will outline that before you book treatment.

Is a bridge better than an implant?

It depends on the bone, the neighbouring teeth, and your budget. We can compare the options at your appointment.
