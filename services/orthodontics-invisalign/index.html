Invisalign and orthodontics in Edmonton

Crooked teeth or a bite that bothers you? We can talk through Invisalign and other orthodontic options at our Queen Alexandra clinic.

Call (780) 435-5300 Book / request an appointment

Clear aligners and when they fit

Invisalign-style aligners suit many mild to moderate cases. Some bites need a different plan. We will not start aligners until we have records and a clear goal.

Adults and older teens near campus often ask about this. Wear time and retainers matter. Results depend on following the plan.

What to ask at a consultation

Bring any previous orthodontic records. Ask whether aligners are suitable for your teeth and bite, whether another approach or a referral would be appropriate, and which dentist would oversee your care.

Understand the full plan

Before starting, ask about the total estimate, appointment schedule, what happens if an aligner is lost, and whether retainers and follow-up are included. Treatment length and daily wear instructions depend on your individual plan.
