Patient guide

Sleep apnea and dental appliance questions

Questions about snoring or a sleep appliance should start with a proper diagnosis and coordination with your medical provider.

Sleep care is different from sedation

This page concerns sleep-related breathing and oral appliances. If you are asking about being sedated for a dental procedure, call the office to discuss what is available for your treatment.

Start with your medical provider

Sleep apnea treatment depends on the diagnosis and may involve a breathing device, an oral appliance, or other care. Ask your medical provider which options fit your situation. Do not stop prescribed treatment without discussing it with them.

Before choosing an appliance

Bring your sleep-study results and current treatment details. Ask how the appliance would be fitted, monitored, and assessed for effectiveness. If you are asking about a named system such as Vivos, confirm availability and discuss its evidence and suitability with your treating clinicians.

Planning a visit to 109 Dental

Call (780) 435-5300 to ask which appointment to book and whether the care you need is available at the clinic. Bring any referral, recent dental records, medication list, and coverage details. An assessment helps determine the next step, including whether a referral is needed.
