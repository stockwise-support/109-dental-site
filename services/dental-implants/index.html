Dental implants near U of A

Missing a tooth and want a fixed replacement? 109 Dental can assess whether an implant is a sound option for your bone and bite.

Call (780) 435-5300 Book / request an appointment

How implants work in plain terms

An implant is a titanium post placed in the jaw, then a connector and a crown. It can replace one tooth or support more than one, if your health and bone allow it.

Not every patient is a candidate on the first visit. We explain that after x-rays and a health review. Healing takes time. We will explain the stages of treatment before you decide.

Before deciding on an implant

Ask about the assessment, alternatives, stages of treatment, and who would provide each stage. Request an estimate that distinguishes the implant, replacement tooth, and any additional procedures.

Questions

Do you place implants at this clinic?

Dr. Barkwell provides implant care as part of general dentistry. A consult tells us whether you can be treated here or need a specialist.

Does CDCP cover dental implants?

CDCP does not cover dental implants or implant-related procedures. Private insurance benefits vary. Bring your plan details so we can help you check your coverage.
