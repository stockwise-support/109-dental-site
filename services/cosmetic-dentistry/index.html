Cosmetic dentistry in Edmonton

If you want a brighter or more even smile, start with an exam at our 109 Street clinic. Whitening, bonding, and veneers are options we discuss after we see your teeth.

Call (780) 435-5300 Book / request an appointment

What we can talk through

Whitening, bonding, and veneers are the usual cosmetic requests. Some smiles need a crown or orthodontics first. We will explain the options after examining your teeth.

Ask us about the expected results, upkeep, and cost of each option.

Whitening, veneers, and bonding

Ask which option addresses your concern, whether any tooth preparation is needed, and what maintenance or replacement could involve. Discuss the likely result and limitations before deciding. A photograph alone cannot establish which treatment is suitable.

Gum shape and your smile

If you are asking about a gum lift or laser gum contouring, start by discussing your gum health and the result you want. Ask whether the procedure is available for your case and whether a referral is needed. A particular technique is not suitable for everyone.

Questions

Can I book whitening as a new patient?

Yes, but we still need a checkup first so we are not bleaching over decay or gum disease.

Do you offer veneers?

Veneers can be discussed when they are a fit. They are not the first answer for every chip or colour concern.
