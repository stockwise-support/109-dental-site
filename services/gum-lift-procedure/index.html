Gum contouring and gum lift questions

A change to the gum line should start with an assessment of gum health and the cause of the concern.

What is gum contouring?

Gum contouring changes the shape of gum tissue around teeth. The terms gum lift and laser gum contouring can describe related procedures, but the instrument does not establish whether treatment is suitable.

Cosmetic concern or gum disease?

Gum swelling, recession and the amount of gum visible when smiling have different causes. They should not be treated as the same problem. An examination helps identify what is happening and whether gum disease needs attention first.

Before agreeing to treatment

Ask what tissue would be changed, whether the supporting bone is involved, and whether specialist assessment is appropriate. Discuss the intended result, healing, risks and alternatives. Confirm availability and the treating provider rather than assuming a laser procedure is offered.

Estimate and recovery

Request a written estimate and instructions specific to the proposed procedure. Ask when to return for review and who to call about bleeding, pain or other concerns. Cosmetic treatment may not be covered by insurance.

Planning a visit to 109 Dental

Call (780) 435-5300 to ask which appointment to book and whether the care you need is available at the clinic. Bring any referral, recent dental records, medication list, and coverage details. An assessment helps determine the next step, including whether a referral is needed.
