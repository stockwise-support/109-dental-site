Patient guide

Teeth whitening: what to consider

Whitening changes the colour of natural teeth. An examination helps explain what results are realistic.

Will whitening work on my teeth?

Different causes of discolouration respond differently. Crowns, veneers and fillings do not whiten like natural teeth, so existing dental work can affect the final colour match.

Before choosing a product

Discuss tooth sensitivity, gum health and any visible restorations. Decay or other problems may need attention first. Tell the dentist about whitening products you already use and any irritation they have caused.

What about sensitivity?

Whitening can cause temporary sensitivity or irritation. Follow the instructions for the specific product and ask what to do if symptoms develop. More frequent use is not a substitute for following the recommended routine.

Which treatment and how much?

Ask which options are currently offered, what is included, and whether trays or follow-up are separate. Do not assume a particular brand or a fixed shade change. Discuss the total estimate and any limits of insurance coverage before starting.

Planning a visit to 109 Dental

Call (780) 435-5300 to ask which appointment to book and whether the care you need is available at the clinic. Bring any referral, recent dental records, medication list, and coverage details. An assessment helps determine the next step, including whether a referral is needed.
