Patient guide

Braces and orthodontic choices

Braces and clear aligners move teeth under a supervised treatment plan.

Braces or clear aligners?

Fixed braces use brackets and wires; clear aligners use a series of removable appliances. Suitability depends on the movement needed, your bite and your ability to follow the treatment routine. An assessment is needed before choosing.

How long does treatment take?

There is no single timetable for every patient. The planned changes, oral health and progress at follow-up visits affect the schedule. Ask for a proposed range and what could extend it.

What should an estimate cover?

Ask about records, appliances, routine visits, repairs, refinements and retainers. Confirm whether treatment is provided at the clinic or through a referral and which appliance options are currently available.

Cleaning and retention

Brackets and wires need careful daily cleaning. Removable aligners also require a consistent cleaning and wear routine. After active treatment, retainers help maintain the result; ask about their wear schedule and replacement costs before starting.

Planning a visit to 109 Dental

Call (780) 435-5300 to ask which appointment to book and whether the care you need is available at the clinic. Bring any referral, recent dental records, medication list, and coverage details. An assessment helps determine the next step, including whether a referral is needed.
