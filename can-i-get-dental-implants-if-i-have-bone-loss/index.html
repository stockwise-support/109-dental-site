Dental implants and bone loss: what to ask

An implant assessment can help you understand whether additional evaluation or treatment is needed before replacing a tooth.

Start with an assessment

An implant needs support in the jaw. An examination and appropriate imaging help the dentist assess the available bone and plan care. A previous diagnosis of bone loss does not, on its own, tell you which treatment is suitable.

Discuss the whole treatment plan

Ask whether bone grafting needs to be considered, who would carry out each stage, and whether a specialist assessment is appropriate. Ask about healing time, possible risks, and alternatives before committing.

Ask for an itemized estimate

Check whether the estimate includes imaging, any preliminary procedures, the implant, the replacement tooth, and follow-up. CDCP does not cover implants or implant-related procedures. Private coverage varies.

Planning a visit to 109 Dental

Call (780) 435-5300 to ask which appointment to book and whether the care you need is available at the clinic. Bring any referral, recent dental records, medication list, and coverage details. An assessment helps determine the next step, including whether a referral is needed.
