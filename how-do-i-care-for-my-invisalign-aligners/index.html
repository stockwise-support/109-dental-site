Patient guide

Looking after your Invisalign aligners

Keep your dentist's instructions handy and ask before changing how you clean, wear, or replace an aligner.

Follow the instructions for your appliance

Use the cleaning method and wear schedule supplied for your aligners. Ask the dental team which products are appropriate. Do not assume instructions for a retainer, denture, or another brand apply to your aligners.

Ask before moving to the next set

If an aligner is lost, damaged, or no longer fits, contact the treating office. Explain which set you are wearing and when the problem started. Ask whether to use the previous set or wait for a replacement rather than deciding on your own.

Keep follow-up appointments

Bring the questions you have about fit, cleaning, eating, or wear time. Tell the dentist if you have been unable to follow the schedule. At the end of treatment, ask about your retainer and ongoing checks.

Planning a visit to 109 Dental

Call (780) 435-5300 to ask which appointment to book and whether the care you need is available at the clinic. Bring any referral, recent dental records, medication list, and coverage details. An assessment helps determine the next step, including whether a referral is needed.
