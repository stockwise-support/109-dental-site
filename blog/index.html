Before your visit

Questions worth asking.

Learn about your options and bring your questions to the appointment. Your dentist can explain what applies to you.

Planning for a family dental emergency

Know who to call, what information to have ready, and when an appointment request cannot wait.

Looking after your Invisalign aligners

Keep your dentist's instructions handy and ask before changing how you clean, wear, or replace an aligner.

Is a dental bridge right for a missing tooth?

A bridge is one way to replace a missing tooth. Compare its support, upkeep, and cost with the other options available to you.

Dental implants and bone loss: what to ask

An implant assessment can help you understand whether additional evaluation or treatment is needed before replacing a tooth.

Questions about bone grafting before implants

If bone grafting has been mentioned in your treatment plan, ask why it is being considered and how it affects the next steps.

Considering dentures

Ask about replacing missing teeth, adjusting to a denture, and how it compares with a bridge or implant.

Questions about gum disease and treatment

Bleeding or sore gums are worth discussing at a dental visit. An assessment helps identify what care is needed.

Receding gums and gum grafting questions

If you notice a change in your gum line or tooth sensitivity, ask for an assessment before choosing a procedure.

Jaw pain and TMJ questions

Pain, locking, or difficulty moving your jaw deserves an assessment. A treatment name alone does not establish the cause.

Sleep apnea and dental appliance questions

Questions about snoring or a sleep appliance should start with a proper diagnosis and coordination with your medical provider.

Tongue-tie and frenectomy questions

A tight band of tissue does not always need treatment. Assessment should focus on the difficulties a person is experiencing.

Children's growth and orthodontic questions

Ask about crowding, bite development, and the right time for an orthodontic assessment.

Digital scans and planning dental treatment

If a digital scan is part of your proposed treatment, ask what it records and how it will be used.

Dental checkups in Edmonton

A checkup looks at the health of your teeth and gums and helps you plan any care you need.

Teeth cleaning and hygiene visits

Understand what a hygiene appointment includes and how it differs from treatment for gum disease.

Dental fillings and bonding

Fillings repair damaged tooth structure. Bonding can also change the shape or appearance of a tooth.

Dental crowns: repair and treatment planning

A crown covers a tooth to restore its shape and function when a smaller repair may not be enough.

Dental bridges for missing teeth

A bridge fills a gap left by missing teeth and is supported by teeth or implants.

Teeth whitening: what to consider

Whitening changes the colour of natural teeth. An examination helps explain what results are realistic.

Dental veneers: benefits and limits

Veneers cover the front surface of teeth to change their appearance.

Braces and orthodontic choices

Braces and clear aligners move teeth under a supervised treatment plan.

Gum contouring and gum lift questions

A change to the gum line should start with an assessment of gum health and the cause of the concern.

Infant tongue-tie and feeding concerns

Feeding difficulties need an assessment of the baby and feeding process, not just the appearance of a tongue-tie.
